Good questions

Before you join.

What if I don't want to reveal my photos?

Then you don't have to. Photos only become visible when both people agree, so nobody can reveal you without your say.

Who can see my profile?

Your details won't appear on a public profile. You can use a nickname, and your identity stays yours until you're comfortable sharing it.

Who can join the early access list?

Early access is aimed at students in Gaborone. Choose your university and use an email address you can access. We may verify student status separately before launch.

What happens to my details?

We'll never sell your personal information. For the waitlist we only ask for your email, your university and, if you like, your gender.
